## PART 3: WRITING STYLE GUIDE

---

### SENTENCE LENGTH

| Type | Target Length | Usage |
|------|--------------|-------|
| **Headlines** | 3–8 words | Value propositions, section titles, CTAs |
| **Subheadings** | 10–20 words | Supporting context for headlines |
| **Body sentences** | 12–22 words | Explanations, descriptions, process steps |
| **Impact sentences** | 3–7 words | Stats, proof points, tag pills |

**Rule of thumb:** Alternate short sentences with medium sentences. Never use three long sentences in a row. End sections on short sentences.

**Good pattern:**
> 150+ projects. Five years of execution. Every one delivered. (short, short, medium, short)

**Bad pattern:**
> We have been working in the content marketing space for over five years and during that time we have successfully completed more than one hundred and fifty projects for clients across a wide range of industries. (single 40-word sentence)

---

### VOCABULARY

**Words Growthscape uses:**

| Category | Preferred Words |
|----------|----------------|
| **Action** | build, engineer, deliver, execute, ship, scale, compound, optimize, iterate |
| **Measurement** | data, metrics, results, outcomes, growth, ROI, performance, signals |
| **System** | system, loop, process, framework, structure, engine, architecture |
| **Partnership** | partner, team, together, we, your brand, your growth |
| **Quality** | precise, sharp, focused, intentional, strategic, cinematic, premium |
| **Time** | monthly, quarterly, consistent, compounding, repeatable, sustainable |

**Words Growthscape avoids:**

| Category | Avoid | Use Instead |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| **Hype** | amazing, incredible, world-class, revolutionary, game-changing | specific (150+ projects, 99% satisfaction) |
| **Vague** | various, multiple, several, many, some | specific numbers |
| **Corporate jargon** | leverage, synergize, holistic, ecosystem,赋能 | plain English |
| **Buzzwords** | disruption, innovation, AI-powered, next-gen | describe what it actually does |
| **Weak qualifiers** | we think, we believe, hopefully, trying to | state it directly |
| **Organic/nature** | organic, natural, bloom, flourish, seed, cultivate | Growthscape is not Organic — avoid its vocabulary |

---

### READING LEVEL

**Target:** Grade 8–10 (Flesch-Kincaid). Accessible to a busy founder skimming on their phone.

**How to hit it:**
- Use active voice (80%+ of sentences)
- Prefer simple words over complex ones
- Keep paragraphs to 1–3 sentences
- Use lists and bullets for scannable content
- Avoid clauses within clauses

**Good:**
> We build content systems. You get the growth. (Grade 4)

**Bad:**
> By leveraging our comprehensive content marketing infrastructure, we are able to facilitate measurable growth trajectories for brands seeking to amplify their digital presence. (Grade 18)

### CONFIDENCE

**Confidence level:** High. Growthscape states facts, not opinions.

**Rules:**
1. **Never say "we believe" or "we think."** Say "this works" or "the data shows."
2. **Never say "we try."** Say "we do" or "we deliver."
3. **Never say "best."** Say "150+ projects" or "99% satisfaction" — let the number speak.
4. **Never hedge with "may" or "might."** Say "will" or "does."
5. **Never apologize for the price.** The value is in the system, not the cost.

**Confidence examples:**

| Weak | Growthscape |
|------|------------|
| "We think we can help your brand grow" | "We build content systems that compound" |
| "We try to deliver high-quality content" | "Every piece is strategy-first, production-grade" |
| "We believe in organic growth" | "200+ brands grew without paid media" |
| "Our team works hard" | "150+ projects delivered. 99% satisfaction." |

### CTA STYLE

**CTA philosophy:** CTAs should feel like the next logical step, not a sales pitch.

**CTA hierarchy:**

| Level | Style | Example |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **Primary** | Action-oriented, direct | "Start Your Growth System" |
| **Secondary** | Exploratory, lower commitment | "See Our Work" |
| **Tertiary** | Informational | "Learn More" |

**CTA rules:**
1. **Verb first.** "Start" not "Get started." "See" not "Check out."
2. **Specific over generic.** "Start Your Growth System" not "Learn More."
3. **Low-pressure where possible.** "15 minutes. No pitch deck." not "Book Your FREE Consultation NOW!!!"
4. **Match the section energy.** High-energy sections get direct CTAs. Low-energy sections get gentle CTAs.
5. **Never use "click here."**

**CTA examples by section:**

| Section | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA |
|---------|------------|---------------|
| **Hero** | Start Your Growth System | See Our Work |
| **About** | — | — |
| **Process** | Build Your System | See How It Works |
| **Case Studies** | See the Results | View on Instagram |
| **Packages** | Start Your Growth System | — |
| **Contact** | Send Message | — |
| **Final CTA** | Start Your Growth System | — |
