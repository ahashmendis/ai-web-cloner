- "150+ projects completed"

### Prohibited Claims
- "Best agency" / "World-class" / "Revolutionary"
- "Millions of views" (unverified)
- "Top 10 agency" (unverified)
- Any metric not on the verified list
- Fabricated testimonials or quotes
- Geographic claims not verified (e.g., "Global leader")
- Any Organic-specific vocabulary (organic, natural, bloom, flourish, seed, cultivate)
> *Source: GROWTHSCAPE_BRAND_SYSTEM.md — Vocabulary guide*

### Writing Rules
- Active voice 80%+ of sentences
- Grade 8–10 reading level (Flesch-Kincaid)
- Headlines: 3–8 words. Body: 12–22 words. Impact: 3–7 words.
- Never three long sentences in a row. End sections on short sentences.
- Verb-first CTAs. No "click here." No "learn more" as primary CTA.
> *Source: GROWTHSCAPE_BRAND_SYSTEM.md — Writing Style Guide*
